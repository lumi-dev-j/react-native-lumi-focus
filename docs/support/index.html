Common Topics

Timer isn't working correctly

If your focus, short break, or long break timer seems inaccurate or gets stuck, try closing and reopening the app. If the issue continues, let us know your device model and iOS version.

Ambient sound issues

If ambient sounds aren't playing, check that your device isn't in silent mode and that media volume is turned up. If the problem persists, tell us which scene and sound you were using.

Purchases

If you have a question about a purchase, or believe you were charged incorrectly, contact us with your Apple receipt or order ID and we'll help sort it out.

General feedback

Have an idea for a new scene, sound, or feature? We'd love to hear it. Send us a message anytime.

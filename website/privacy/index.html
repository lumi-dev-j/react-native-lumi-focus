Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Lumi Focus ("the app", "we", "us") is a Pomodoro-style productivity timer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does and does not collect, and how it is used.

Account Creation

Lumi Focus does not currently require you to create an account or sign in to use the app.

Information We Collect

Lumi Focus does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not require your name, email address, or any other identifying information to use the app.

Information Stored On Your Device

The app may save your preferences locally on your device, such as your timer duration settings, selected scene, selected ambient sound, and basic focus session history. This information stays on your device, is never transmitted to us or any third party, and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

Lumi Focus does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not sell or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any to begin with.

Cookies

Lumi Focus does not use cookies. This website does not use cookies or tracking technologies either.

Children's Privacy

Lumi Focus does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because the app does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example as the app adds new features. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
